# Product vision

LifeOS turns scattered life administration into information people can find,
understand and act on. Launch serves individual consumers with a personal
workspace. Families, freelancers, small businesses and teams follow later.

The central journey is capture, organize, review, remember and act. A person
uploads a document, confirms its important details, links a task or reminder,
and sees upcoming responsibilities. AI may accelerate discovery but must never
be required to upload, search metadata, edit records or manage tasks.

Principles: private by default; explicit and reversible choices; evidence before
claims; useful manual paths; accessible universal experience; minimal data
collection; clear distinction between suggestions and confirmed information.

Proposed validation metrics: successful first document retrieval, completion of
first linked reminder, weekly return for administrative work, reminder delivery
reliability and correction rates for extracted fields. Establish baselines in
consented synthetic/internal trials before setting targets. Do not collect raw
document contents or chat text for analytics.

Commercial tiers are hypotheses: Free, Personal, Family and Business. Prices,
quotas, conversion targets and launch regions require validation. The product
does not provide legal, financial or medical determinations, guarantee consumer
rights, or promise to cancel services without verified execution.
